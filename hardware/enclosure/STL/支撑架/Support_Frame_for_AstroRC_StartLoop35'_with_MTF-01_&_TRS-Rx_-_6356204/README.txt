Support Frame for AstroRC StartLoop35' with MTF-01 & TRS-Rx by MicoAir on Thingiverse: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:6356204

Summary:
This AstroRC StarLoop support frame, designed by MicoAir, offers convenient assembly for the MTF-01 lidar &amp; optical flow sensor and TRS-Rx. Printed using LEDO6060 resin material, the frame ensures stability and impact resistance. It is secured through the rack's screw holes, providing stable support for drone take-off and landing.The TRS-Rx serves as both an RC receiver and telemetry device. And the MTF-01 lidar &amp; optical flow sensor enables stable hover and flight in indoor environments. BTW, you can access the module at: https://www.aliexpress.us/item/3256805359467554.html
