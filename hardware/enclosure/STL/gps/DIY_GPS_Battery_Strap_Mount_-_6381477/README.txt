DIY GPS Battery Strap Mount by MicoAir on Thingiverse: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:6381477

Summary:
This is a 3D-printed battery strap GPS mount used in drone to securely attach a GPS module to a battery strap. You can print it using resin or plastic materials, such as TPU and CBY. It supports 20mm20mm and 25mm25mm GPS modules, as well as 15mm and 20mm strap widths. The mounts feature the MicoAir GPS modules: M10G-5883, MG-A01, MG-902 and MG-903. BTW, you can access the GPS module at: https://www.aliexpress.us/item/3256805405708253.html
